About Us

Shri Krishna Enterprises

Shri Krishna Enterprises is an electrical and fire-safety contracting business focused on delivering reliable installation, project execution, and site coordination services for residential, commercial, and industrial projects.

We work with builders, developers, EPC contractors, MEP consultants, facility managers, and other industry partners to support electrical infrastructure and fire-protection requirements. Our approach emphasizes quality workmanship, safe execution, technical coordination, and timely delivery in accordance with the approved project scope and applicable standards.

From electrical installation packages to fire alarm and fire-fighting system works, we aim to provide practical, coordinated solutions that support project requirements from execution through testing and handover, wherever included in our scope.
